Why Food Rescue exists

Making food donation easier to coordinate.

We give donors and recipients one practical place to list, discover, request, and track surplus food.

01

The problem

Restaurants, hostels, hotels, events, cafeterias, and households can have perfectly edible surplus without a reliable way to find a recipient in time.

02

Our solution

A centralized, time-aware platform for listing surplus, discovering nearby donations, coordinating requests, and tracking every handoff.

03

Our mission

Reduce avoidable food waste and make local donation coordination feel simple, dignified, and accountable.
